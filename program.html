11:00 – 12:30
SAE x Abbey Road x CvA Panel Talks
Alicia & Carla Room

How Can Education Impact the Future of the Music Industry?

As the music industry continues to evolve, how can education help shape its future?

In this panel discussion, Abbey Road Institute, SAE Institute and AEMA (Amsterdam Electronic Music Academy), part of the Conservatorium van Amsterdam, will explore the role of education in developing the next generation of artists, songwriters and producers.

The discussion will cover three key themes:

  • The impact of music education on the industry.
  • How new technologies, including AI, and changing workflows are reshaping music production.
  • How education can prepare emerging professionals for sustainable careers in music.

Bringing together different perspectives from music education, the panel aims to encourage meaningful conversations about the challenges and opportunities ahead.

The Day Program

Industry conversations, meetings, networking and creator activity across the hotel. Open to confirmed Industry Hub registrants; individual sessions may carry their own conditions.

  • Industry conversations & keynotes — Oscar Room
  • Innovation sessions — Meet Me Innovation stage
  • Brand activations throughout the hotel
  • Industry Exchange meetings — pre-booked via the Exchange
  • Creator sessions — live podcast & video from the hotel floor
  • Open networking — Aitana Lounge · Industry Hub

The Night Program

Partner activations and curated experiences across the hotel venues. Access is arranged per session: a request confirmed by the organising partner, a personal invitation, or public tickets for the family showcases. See the timetable above for the conditions per session.

  • OSCAR ROOM — Partner showcases, label nights & branded evenings · 132m² marina stage
  • AITANA LOUNGE — Networking receptions, partner gatherings & branded activations from 18:00
  • HOTEL SUITES — Private industry meetings, hosted conversations & closed-door sessions
  • IJDOK — Boat events departing from the marina · by invitation · Private IJ canal experiences

The Private Access

Closed-door sessions, executive roundtables, invite-only dinners and private conversations for senior industry professionals. Smaller groups, deeper dialogue.

  • Executive roundtables — 12 person max
  • Investor sessions — closed door
  • Industry Dinner — by invitation (date to be confirmed)
  • Label Office Hours — booking agent desk
  • By partnership agreement or invitation
